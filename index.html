FCA Enforcement Tracker

The False Claims Act turns a bad self-assessment into fraud. DOJ's Civil Cyber-Fraud Initiative has been settling cybersecurity cases since 2021. Every case below is individually sourced. The pattern is the point: none of these started with a breach. They started with a score that did not hold up.

Why this matters to you

  • Treble damages. The FCA allows triple the government's losses plus per-claim penalties. Settlements are the discount; trial is the multiplier.
  • Whistleblowers. Most of these cases started with an employee filing a qui tam suit. Your own team knows your real score.
  • Your SPRS score is evidence. DOJ does not need to prove you were hacked. It needs to show the score was false and you kept billing.
  • Self-disclosure pays. Verizon cooperated and self-reported; its penalty multiplier dropped from 3x to 1.5x, saving over $1.3 million.
  • Buyers inherit history. Nightwing paid $8.4 million for conduct that predated its acquisition. Check cybersecurity representations in due diligence.
